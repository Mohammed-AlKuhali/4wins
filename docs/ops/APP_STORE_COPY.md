**Description**

```
Most habit apps reward streaks. 4Wins rewards discipline.

Every day, you log one meaningful win across four pillars of a life well-lived:

  Mental — read, learn, think deeply
  Financial — save, track, build
  Spiritual — reflect, pray, meditate
  Physical — move, rest, nourish

Close all four quadrants and the ring seals. That's your win.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the app each morning
• Log your four daily wins — by voice or text
• The Quad ring tracks your progress through the day
• At midnight, your day closes — complete or incomplete
• No shame on a rest day. No hiding streaks. Just honesty.

VOICE JOURNALING
Tap the mic and speak. 4Wins transcribes your words and tags the right pillar automatically.

STREAKS WITH INTEGRITY
Day 1 never counts — discipline takes 48 hours to begin. 
Monthly streak freezes let life happen without losing everything.
Zero-shame resets: the app never hides or buries incomplete days.

WEEKLY & MONTHLY REVIEWS
At the end of each week, see your four pillars in review.
At 30 days, get a personal insight report on your patterns.

YOUR TRADITION, YOUR WAY
4Wins adapts to how you see the Spiritual pillar — Christian, Stoic, Buddhist, secular, or your own custom practice.

PRIVACY FIRST
No ads. No social feed. No public leaderboards. 
Voice notes are transcribed and discarded — never stored as audio.
Health data (steps, workouts) is read on-device and never leaves your phone.

Start with 14 days free. Then $4.99/month or $39.99/year.
```
